About Tim and the GouMang Team

GouMang, 句芒, is the god who governs trees and spring in the 《山海经》, the ancient Chinese Classic of Mountains and Seas. It is a fitting reminder that every piece begins with a living material, and that renewal is part of making something lasting.

GouMang Creations (句芒木作) started in 2021 as a small weekend project but quickly grew into a lifelong passion.

Our unwavering dedication to creating heirloom furnitures and extraordinary craftsmanship is what drives us every day to create special peices for our customers that will last for generations.

Based in Tucson, Arizona Northern Virginia, we believe in sustainable and eco-friendly practices, sourcing only the finest materials for our pieces. Each item is meticulously crafted to ensure it not only meets but exceeds expectations.

When we are not in the workshop, we're out exploring the breathtaking Sonoran Desert, drawing inspiration from the state's natural splendor, rich indigenous culture, and historic Asian heritage. Our journey is just beginning, and we are excited to have you to be a part of it.
