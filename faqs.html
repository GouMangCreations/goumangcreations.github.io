A note before we begin

Questions
worth asking.

A custom piece starts with a conversation. Here are a few things people often want to know before we begin.

Starting a commission

How does a custom commission begin?

Tell us what you need, where the piece will live, and what you have in mind. A room photo, rough dimensions, and a few reference images can help. We’ll talk through possible designs and materials, then provide a free quote before you decide whether to move forward. Email about your project ↗, or message us on Instagram ↗.

Can you make a piece to fit my space?

Yes. Dimensions and details can be developed around your room and how you plan to use the piece. The design, materials, and labor all affect the quote, so we’ll discuss the scope before work begins.

How long does a commission take?

Timing depends on the design, materials, and current workshop schedule. We’ll give you an estimate for your specific project before you decide. Please ask for an updated timeline when you reach out.

How much does handmade furniture cost?

Each piece is different. Size, wood species, design complexity, joinery, finish, and delivery all affect the price. Once we understand the project, we can provide a quote with the scope clearly described.

Materials & making

Which wood should I choose?

That depends on the piece, the room, how it will be used, and the look you’re drawn to. We can compare species, grain, and finish together. Our materials guide is a useful place to start.

Can I choose a stain or a particular color?

We prefer to begin with the natural color and character of the wood. Stain can hide variation and figure, though color goals are welcome in the conversation. We can discuss suitable species and finish samples for your project.

Do I need to choose the joinery?

No. We choose joints to suit the structure, material, and design. If you especially like a visible detail such as dovetails, tell us: we can discuss whether and where it makes sense. Read more in our joinery note.

Is every piece exactly the same?

Wood varies naturally in color, grain, and figure, so no two boards are identical. We select and arrange boards with care, but those natural differences are part of the finished piece.

Receiving & care

How is delivery arranged?

Delivery options and costs depend on the finished piece and destination. We’ll discuss them as part of your quote and confirm the arrangements before delivery. Please mention stairs, narrow doorways, or other access details early.

How should I care for my furniture?

Care depends on the wood and finish. In general, dust with a soft cloth, wipe spills promptly, and avoid prolonged moisture or direct heat. We’ll share care guidance suited to your piece and its finish.

Can a handmade piece be repaired or refinished?

Many solid wood pieces can be maintained, repaired, or refinished, depending on their construction and condition. Get in touch with photos and a description, and we can talk through what may be possible.
