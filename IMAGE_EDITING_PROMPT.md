# GouMang Creations studio-product-photo prompt

Use the **Oakton shoe rack studio image** as the style reference and process one source photo at a time. The input photo is the source of truth for the woodworking piece.

## Main prompt

> Transform this reference photo of a handmade GouMang Creations furniture piece into a high-end, photorealistic studio product photograph. Match the visual treatment of the provided Oakton shoe-rack reference: an off-white to pure-white seamless cyclorama background, a clean matte studio floor, soft diffused light from the upper left, gentle grounding shadow beneath the piece, natural warm wood tones, and a quiet premium furniture-catalog aesthetic. Isolate the entire furniture piece from its original environment and center it with generous white negative space. Retain the source photo’s camera angle and preserve the **exact object** without redesigning it: every proportion, live edge, grain pattern, joinery, dovetail, inlay, hardware, shelf, cut-out, finish, mark, and construction detail must remain faithful to the source. Correct only exposure, white balance, perspective, and distracting background elements. Keep the full piece in frame; do not crop any leg, corner, or edge. Photorealistic commercial product photography, sharp but natural details, realistic contact shadow, sRGB.

## Detail-shot variant

Use this when the source is a close-up rather than a full-piece image:

> Create a photorealistic studio detail photograph of this handmade GouMang Creations furniture piece. Replace the environment with an off-white seamless backdrop and soft, diffused product-studio lighting. Preserve the exact crop, camera angle, wood grain, joinery, finish, and construction details from the source image. Do not add or remove any feature, alter the shape, change the wood species, smooth away tool marks, or invent hardware. Keep the subject sharp with a subtle, natural falloff into the clean background; use soft grounding shadow only where the piece meets the floor or surface.

## Negative prompt / constraints

> No redesign, no altered dimensions, no additional shelves or joinery, no changed wood grain, no fake veneer, no plastic-looking finish, no excessive gloss, no HDR, no oversaturation, no blur, no text, no watermark, no props, no plants, no workshop setting, no room interior, no outdoor deck, no people, no pets, no clipped legs or edges, no floating object, no harsh or multiple shadows.

## Export and review

- Keep the original aspect ratio whenever possible; retain the full piece with comfortable border space.
- Export sRGB JPEG at 1800px on the long edge, roughly 80–85% quality.
- Compare the result beside the source at 100% before publishing. Reject outputs that change any construction or material detail.
